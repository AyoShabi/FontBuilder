FontBuilder

Make a font for your brand by redrawing an open-licence font with Illustrator's own tools.

This is a preview. Inside Illustrator, open it from Window > Extensions > FontBuilder to create a canvas.

1. Choose a starting font

Only TrueType (.ttf) fonts under the SIL Open Font License or Apache License can be changed. Adobe Fonts and most fonts you buy can't.

2. Set up your canvas

FontBuilder makes a new document with one artboard per letter. Each artboard is as wide as the letter's spacing, with guides for the baseline, x-height, cap height, ascender and descender. You'll work through the stages in this order:

Your letters

Click a letter to jump to its artboard. Draw on the Letters layer with any of Illustrator's tools; the guides are on a locked layer.
